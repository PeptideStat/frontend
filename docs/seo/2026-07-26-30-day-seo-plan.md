# PeptideStat 30-Day SEO Plan

**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Site:** https://www.peptidestat.com  
**Goal:** Move from “indexed but invisible” to ranking impressions on 10–15 competitive queries, by fixing depth, E-E-A-T, cannibalization, and off-site signals — not by publishing more thin pages.

**Constraint:** Freeze net-new compound pages unless they close a clear gap on the priority list. Quality over volume.

---

| Metric | Baseline (assume low) | Target |
|--------|----------------------|--------|

| Bing Webmaster + GSC fully verified | unknown | Both live; sitemaps submitted |

Track weekly in a simple sheet: URL × primary query × impressions × clicks × avg position.

---

## North-star principles

1. **One primary URL per intent.** Guides win narrative/how-to; database wins structured “what is X / half-life / status” fact lookups.
2. **Depth on money pages first.** Thin pillars (BPC-157, retatrutide, dosage) are the emergency.
3. **YMYL trust before affiliate.** Evidence, sources, reviewer → then partner card.
4. **No more mass publish.** Every new page must justify a unique query + unique outline.
5. **Off-site is half the job.** Content alone will not beat medical publishers.

## Part B — Page expansion template (use for every Tier 1–2 rewrite)

### Required structure

1. **Direct answer in first 100–150 words** (what it is, status, one-line takeaway).
2. **Regulatory / evidence status box** (approved / investigational / research-only; human vs animal).
3. **Mechanism** (plain language + targets).
4. **Evidence table** (study | design | N | dose | outcome | limitations | source link).
5. **Dosing context** (trial protocols only; never “recommended dose for you”).
6. **Side effects / safety** (what labels/trials report vs what is unknown).
7. **Comparisons** (link to dedicated vs pages; short summary here).
8. **Who this page is for / not for**.
9. **FAQ** (5–8 real questions; keep frontmatter `faqs` for schema).
10. **Sources** (primary: NEJM, FDA labels, ClinicalTrials.gov, PMC — not other blogs).
11. **Last reviewed date** (`updated` frontmatter) + reviewer byline.
12. **Partner card only after substance** (existing `ArticlePartnerCard` placement: mid/low, not above fold of thin intros).

### Depth targets

- Thin pillars (BPC, retatrutide hub, dosage): **≥ 2,500 words of non-fluff** or equivalent density via tables + sourced sections.
- Comparisons: decision tables + “choose X if / choose Y if”.
- Every claim that could affect health decisions: **citation or “unknown / not established.”**

### Do not

- Invent dosing protocols as medical advice.
- Rewrite the same paragraph across 6 retatrutide URLs (unique intro + unique sections; share tables via clear “full chart on X” links).
- Keyword-stuff titles; keep current clear title style.

### Outline scraps for the three critical thins

**`/peptides/bpc-157`**

- What BPC-157 is (sequence origin, research compound)
- Why marketing is loud vs evidence
- Preclinical findings by tissue type (table)
- Human evidence (almost none — be explicit)
- WADA / regulatory / compounding context
- Common anecdotal protocols vs “not established”
- Stacks (TB-500) → link comparison page
- Safety unknowns
- FAQ: oral vs injectable, local vs systemic, legal status

**`/peptides/retatrutide`**

- What it is + triple agonist in plain language
- Phase 2 NEJM numbers with caveats
- Phase 3 / TRIUMPH status (update with latest public data)
- Side-effect profile summary → deep page
- Dosage summary → dosage page
- vs tirzepatide / semaglutide → comparison pages
- Approval outlook (no hype dates)
- FAQ

**`/peptides/retatrutide-dosage`**

- “No approved dose” upfront
- Phase 2 escalation arms table (1–12 mg, weekly)
- What Phase 3 used when public
- Why clinics/blogs invent 0.5 mg starts (label that as non-trial folklore)
- Missed dose / titration principles from trials only
- Calculator links
- FAQ: starting dose, max dose, how often, reconstitution

- Link to `/editorial-policy`

JSON-LD: prefer `Person` for author when named; keep Organization as publisher.

### 3. Sitewide trust pages (refresh, don’t invent fiction)

| Page | Action |
|------|--------|

### 5. Experience signals (optional but strong)

## Part D — Cannibalization: `/peptides/*` vs `/database/*`

### Role split (non-negotiable)

| Surface | Wins for | Content shape |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| **`/peptides/<slug>`** | Guides, “complete guide,” how-to, comparisons, results, side effects, marketing debunks | Long-form MDX, FAQs, narrative |
| **`/database/<slug>`** | Entity / fact card: status, half-life, targets, dose *reference*, developer, category | Structured fields, short mechanism, links out to guide |

### Rules

1. **Primary commercial/info intent → guide.**  
   Example: “BPC-157” head term → `/peptides/bpc-157` is primary.

2. **Database is supporting entity.**  
   `/database/bpc-157` title/description should read like a **profile**, not a second complete guide.  
   Example titles:  
   - Guide: `BPC-157: Strong Animal Data, Almost No Human Evidence`  
   - DB: `BPC-157 Profile: Status, Half-Life, Targets & Evidence Score`

3. **Cross-link both ways, with hierarchy.**  
   - Database page: prominent CTA “Read full research guide” → `articleSlug`  
   - Guide page: “Structured compound profile” → `/database/<slug>`  
   - Internal links from blogs/homepage: prefer guide for narrative topics.

4. **`articleSlug` hygiene**  
   - Every important peptide should point `articleSlug` at the **best** guide (pillar), not a random long-tail.  
   - Null only when no guide exists.

5. **No duplicate H1 intent**  
   If both pages rank for the same query, tighten DB meta + intro so it is clearly a data sheet, and expand the guide.

6. **Comparisons only live under `/peptides/`**  
   Never create `/database/bpc-157-vs-tb-500`.

7. **Category hubs**  
   - `/database/healing-peptides` = directory of profiles  
   - Narrative “best healing peptides” style content stays in `/peptides/` if/when written

## What the COA Numbers Actually Establish

The reviewed reports show why an exact lot is more useful than a generic
"99%+" badge. Two R-10 reports on Ascension's site gave different results:
98.863% for Kovera lot 03-05260628 and 99.94% for MZ Biolabs lot
03-01260229. Both documents identify a specific sample and method; neither
supports a claim that every Ascension product has the same purity.

The BPC-157 report adds another useful detail. It records both 99.79% purity
and a measured quantity of 7.89mg for the vial labeled 5mg, plus
HPLC-MS identity confirmation. That is more informative than purity alone
because purity, identity and measured quantity answer different questions.

These records support a favorable **documentation** assessment. They do not
prove pharmacy-grade manufacturing, establish the condition of an unopened
customer vial or make a research product appropriate for human use.

The practical move is simple: before ordering, open the COA page and look
for the product you are considering. When the vial arrives, the lot on the
label should match a public certificate. If it does not, contact support
before opening or using it for any research workflow.

## What a COA Proves, and What It Does Not

A good COA is a strong sourcing signal, but it is not a magic certificate
for every possible use. Synthetic peptide quality can involve closely
related impurities such as truncated sequences, deletion sequences,
stereochemical impurities and degradation products. Analytical methods like
HPLC and LC-MS matter because peptide impurities can be structurally close
to the target compound.

So the right interpretation is:

- A batch-specific COA supports Ascension's credibility as a research source.
- A recent 99% purity result is a meaningful quality signal.
- Lot matching is better than trusting a generic product-page claim.
- A research COA is not the same as FDA approval, sterile pharmacy
  dispensing or medical supervision.

That last sentence is not a knock on Ascension. It is the category boundary.
If you want FDA-approved semaglutide or tirzepatide, read
[FDA-approved GLP-1 drugs](/peptides/fda-approved-glp-1-for-weight-loss) or
[where to get GLP-1 online](/peptides/where-to-get-glp-1-online). Ascension
is a research-peptide source, and it is strongest when judged by research
source standards.

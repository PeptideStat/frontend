## Why NOVA's documentation stands out

The strongest part of NOVA's site is not a generic "99% pure" badge. It is
the structure of the public record.

**The library is searchable by product or batch.** The
[lab-results page](https://www.nova-biolabs.com/lab-results/) places the
product, strength, batch, test date, purity and content in the same row.
That makes it possible to compare a vial label with a named record instead of
searching through an undated PDF folder.

**The records distinguish purity from measured content.** NOVA's BPC-157
entry reports 99.6% purity and 10.45mg content for a nominal 10mg vial. Those
numbers answer different questions. Purity describes the proportion of the
analyzed material attributed to the target compound under the stated assay;
content is the measured quantity in the submitted sample.

**Some entries link more than one test type.** The current GLP-3 (RT) 10mg
row includes purity/content reporting and separate pass entries for heavy
metals, endotoxins and sterility. That is a more informative public trail
than purity alone, although the reports still relate to the vendor-submitted
sample.

**The lab can be checked from the listing.** The BPC-157 product page links
to Janoshik, while the GHK-Cu batch row links to Uzorak. Direct verification
links are preferable to screenshots because the reader can inspect the
laboratory record rather than relying only on NOVA's summary.

There is still an important limit: the vendor controls the product page and
the sample-submission process. The named laboratory reports the sample it
received. PeptideStat did not observe sampling or establish that the report
represents every unit offered under the same listing.

## UAE delivery and GCC shipping

NOVA's [dedicated shipping
policy](https://www.nova-biolabs.com/shipping-policy/) is unusually specific
for this category. It separates local cooled delivery from standard regional
shipping:

| Destination | Published delivery terms |
| --- | --- |
| Dubai and Sharjah | AED 30; free from AED 400; estimated one day; cooled and tracked |
| Other non-western Emirates | AED 60; free from AED 800; estimated one day; cooled and tracked |
| UAE western regions | AED 60; free from AED 800; estimated two to three working days; not cooled |
| Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar and Saudi Arabia | Tracked courier; estimated five working days; no cooled shipping |

The policy says orders are typically processed within one working day after
payment confirmation. It also says customs duties or import taxes on GCC
orders are the recipient's responsibility and that temperature control
cannot be guaranteed after courier handoff.

One inconsistency is worth recording. The dedicated shipping policy lists
free delivery from **AED 400 for Dubai and Sharjah**, while NOVA's FAQ says
shipping is free across the UAE over **AED 800**. We treat the dedicated
policy as the more specific source, but readers should verify the threshold
at checkout or with NOVA before relying on it.

The practical regional distinction is clear: NOVA advertises cooled delivery
for most of the UAE, but not for western UAE regions or GCC destinations.
Calling the entire UAE/GCC service "cold chain" would overstate the published
terms.

## Payment and support

NOVA's FAQ lists credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Tamara,
cryptocurrency and Cash on Delivery. The FAQ says COD carries a 5% handling
fee with a minimum of AED 20 and may depend on the product format or customer
eligibility shown at checkout.

The shipping policy publishes a UAE contact email, WhatsApp number and
weekday support hours. Those details do not prove product quality, but they
create a clearer local support path than a storefront with only an anonymous
contact form.

We have not tested NOVA's response time, returns process or delivery

## What is favorable - and what remains unverified

### Strengths

**Strong public batch trail.** Exact identifiers, dates, purity, measured
content and verification links are the main reason NOVA receives a favorable
documentation assessment.

**A distinct UAE/GCC proposition.** AED pricing and local delivery terms make
NOVA materially different from the US vendors in the PeptideStat directory.

**Useful multi-test reporting on the current GLP-3 row.** Publishing separate
heavy-metals, endotoxin and sterility entries adds context beyond a single
purity percentage.

**Clear research-use language.** NOVA states that its products are for
in-vitro laboratory research and not for human consumption, diagnosis or
treatment.

### Limitations

**No PeptideStat-commissioned test.** Our assessment is based on public pages
and linked reports.

**No independent chain-of-custody finding.** We cannot establish that a
vendor-submitted laboratory sample represents every vial shipped to a
customer.

**Regional temperature-control limits.** The shipping policy excludes cooled
delivery for western UAE regions and GCC countries.

**Customs and duty exposure for GCC orders.** The recipient is responsible
for applicable duties or import taxes, and the policy describes limitations
when customs holds or returns a shipment.

**A commercial relationship exists.** PeptideStat may earn a referral
commission. That conflict is disclosed so readers can judge the article
against its linked evidence.

## Is NOVA Labs legit?

"Legit" is too broad unless the standard is defined. In PeptideStat's narrow
vendor-audit framework, NOVA performs well on the questions we can answer
from public evidence:

- Is there a current, searchable batch library? **Yes.**
- Do records include exact batch numbers and dates? **Yes.**
- Are purity and measured content shown separately? **Yes, on sampled rows.**
- Are external laboratory-verification links available? **Yes, on sampled rows.**
- Are shipping regions and research-use restrictions published? **Yes.**

Questions we cannot answer from the website include whether every shipped
vial matches its posted record, how representative vendor-submitted samples
are, and how the service performs across a large number of real orders.

The accurate conclusion is therefore: **NOVA Labs has one of the stronger
public documentation systems among the UAE/GCC research vendors PeptideStat
tracks, with meaningful limits that readers should not ignore.**

## How to check a NOVA listing yourself

Before relying on any product-page claim:

1. Open the exact product page and record the strength, current price and
   visible batch.
2. Search that batch in NOVA's [lab-results
   library](https://www.nova-biolabs.com/lab-results/).
3. Confirm that the product, strength, batch and test date agree.
4. Open the laboratory-verification link and distinguish identity, purity,
   measured content and any separate screening results.
5. Check the delivery policy for your actual destination, especially if it is
   a western UAE region or another GCC country.
6. Save the record and compare the delivered label with the public batch
   before using the material in a research workflow.

If the batch does not match, the report is missing, or checkout terms differ
from the policy, ask the vendor for clarification instead of assuming that a
generic certificate applies.
